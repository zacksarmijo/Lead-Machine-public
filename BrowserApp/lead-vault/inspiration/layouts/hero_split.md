# Hero Split

Two-column above-the-fold hero.

**Left column (55%):**
- Eyebrow label (small caps, 12px, muted color) stating category or location
- H1 — business-specific, outcome-focused, 8-14 words max. NOT a tagline.
- Subhead — 1-2 sentence clarification, 20-24 words
- Two CTAs side by side: primary (filled, accent color) and secondary (outline or link)
- Trust row below CTAs: 3-5 tiny logos OR stars + review count + "since YYYY"

**Right column (45%):**
- Single high-quality image. Real project photo, not stock.
- 16:10 or 4:5 aspect
- Subtle rounded corners (8-12px). No heavy drop shadows.

**Structural rules:**
- Full-height viewport on desktop, stack vertical on mobile
- Sticky top nav with logo left, 4-5 nav items right, phone number + CTA on far right
- Above-the-fold content must fit in 900px viewport height
- NO carousel. NO video background with autoplay.
