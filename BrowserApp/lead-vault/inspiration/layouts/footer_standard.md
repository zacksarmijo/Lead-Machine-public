# Footer Standard

Dark-background closing footer.

**Top row — 3 or 4 columns:**
1. Brand column: logo, 1-line description, social icons (only real profiles)
2. Services column: list of core services as links
3. Contact column: address, phone, email, hours
4. Legal/Quick links column: privacy, terms, sitemap (if present)

**Bottom row — single line:**
- "© {year} {Business Name}. All rights reserved."
- Small "Website by {you}" credit link on the right (optional)

**Rules:**
- Background: primary or near-black
- Text: muted/light on dark
- Links underline on hover, not by default
- Include structured data (schema.org LocalBusiness) in this section
- NO newsletter signup unless the business actually sends one
- Only link social accounts that were scraped / confirmed real
