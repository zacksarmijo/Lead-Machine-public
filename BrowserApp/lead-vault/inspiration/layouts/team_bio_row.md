# Team Bio Row

Introduce the humans behind the business.

**Layout:**
- Section H2 + optional 1-line lede
- Grid: 2-4 columns depending on team size
- Photo (rounded square, 4:5), name, role, short bio (20-40 words)
- Optional: certifications under role

**Rules:**
- Use REAL photos — no stock agency portraits
- If team photos aren't available, use a single group photo + a paragraph instead of individual cards
- Keep bios outcome-focused: what they do for clients, not just their hobbies
- Certifications listed as real abbreviations (SRES, CSA, CAPS, etc.) — never invented
