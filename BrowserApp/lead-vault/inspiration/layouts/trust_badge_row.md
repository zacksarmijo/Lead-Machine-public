# Trust Badge Row

Single-row band that delivers credibility quickly.

**Contents (pick 3-5, do not exceed 5):**
- Years in business: "Since 1998"
- Certification badges: BBB A+, state license #, industry body
- Review score: "4.9 ★ (127 Google reviews)"
- Insurance/bonded statement
- Notable client logos IF permission obtained

**Layout:**
- Horizontal row, center-aligned on desktop
- Subtle divider between items (vertical line or dot separator)
- Background: surface color (not white) to distinguish from sections above/below
- Vertical padding: 48-64px

**Rules:**
- Do NOT fabricate review counts, years, or certifications
- If data is missing, DROP the section — do not pad with filler
- Badges must be real image assets or SVG; do not fake a BBB logo
