# Emergency Call Band

Conversion band for trade/medical businesses offering urgent service.

**Content:**
- H3 or large copy: "Emergency? Call now." / "24/7 service available."
- Phone number as a massive tappable link (36-48px, bold, underline on hover)
- Response time promise if real: "We answer within 60 seconds."

**Layout:**
- Single row, center-aligned on desktop
- Full-width, accent or warning-colored background for urgency signal
- Vertical padding 48-64px

**Rules:**
- The phone number MUST be a real `tel:+1...` anchor, not just text
- Do NOT claim 24/7 unless confirmed in lead data
- Skip entirely if the business is strictly appointment-only
