# Contact CTA Band

Final conversion band before the footer.

**Content:**
- H2, outcome-focused: "Get a free estimate this week" or "Book a consultation"
- One sentence of reassurance (response time, no-pressure, free, etc.)
- Form OR dual CTA (call + form)

**Form fields (keep minimal — 3-4 max):**
- Name
- Phone OR email (let user choose)
- Message / project type
- Optional: address or zip if service-area relevant

**Rules:**
- Full-width, primary or accent background color
- Text must contrast to WCAG AA
- Form submit handler: point to Formspree / Netlify Forms / Basin — DO NOT build a backend
- Phone number on left, form on right (stack on mobile)
- NO more than 5 form fields. Each extra field kills conversion.
- Privacy microcopy under the form: "We'll never share your info."
