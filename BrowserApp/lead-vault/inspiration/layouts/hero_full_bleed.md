# Hero Full Bleed

Edge-to-edge image or video hero with overlaid content.

**Background:**
- Full-width, 80-90vh tall
- Real project photo (completed home, finished landscape job, etc.) — NOT stock
- Apply a gradient overlay (top-left dark to bottom-right transparent, ~40% opacity) so text reads

**Foreground content — anchor to center or bottom-left:**
- Eyebrow label (uppercase, letter-spaced, 11-13px)
- H1 — 6-12 words, bold display face, 48-72px desktop
- One short subhead line
- Single primary CTA
- Small arrow-down hint to indicate scroll (optional, subtle)

**Rules:**
- Text color must pass WCAG AA contrast on the darkest patch of the image
- Sticky transparent nav becomes opaque on scroll past hero
- NO autoplay audio
- If video: muted, looped, compressed under 2MB, with a static poster frame fallback
